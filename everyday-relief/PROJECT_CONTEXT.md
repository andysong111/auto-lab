# Everyday Relief Studio - Project Context

Last updated: 2026-10-06

## Decision

The owner ended the PlayJolt / LoopJolt game program and redirected the repository to a new AI video
project called `일상시원공감프로젝트`.

The new project creates globally understandable shorts that begin with a familiar emotional trigger
from ordinary life and end with an unpredictable, satisfying release.

## Owner role

The owner should normally do only two things:

1. Supply an idea, situation, or reference.
2. Approve or reject the exact final preview.

Owner feedback during approval becomes durable improvement material for the engine.

## Production loop

1. Intake the owner's idea or reference.
2. Extract the universal emotion without copying protected expression.
3. Produce a deterministic nonverbal story specification.
4. Check safety, originality, language independence, visual clarity, and generation feasibility.
5. Generate a private 9:16 draft.
6. Inspect the actual video for text, speech, continuity, timing, emotional clarity, and catharsis.
7. Repair within the bounded retry budget or reject the draft.
8. Present the exact preview to the owner.
9. After explicit approval, publish through the verified Metricool brand and connected channels.
10. Record retention, replay, sharing, saves, comments, and channel-specific performance.
11. Feed measured winners and owner feedback into the next story specification.

## Content thesis

The repeatable unit is not a topic. It is an emotional shape:

`recognition -> pressure -> expectation -> surprising reversal -> visible relief -> replayable finish`

Initial situation families:

- queue cutting and unfair turns;
- inconsiderate public behavior;
- small household frustrations;
- work or school awkwardness without identifiable organizations;
- loneliness, disappointment, and unnoticed kindness;
- everyday objects that create an unexpected but harmless resolution.

## Global language-free rule

The rendered video must not depend on any language. This includes dialogue, narration, subtitles,
readable signs, readable phone screens, product labels, interface text, logos, and watermarks.

Music, effects, and wordless reactions may carry emotion. The story must remain understandable when
muted.
