# Publishing Contract

## Before generation

- The owner provides an idea or reference.
- The story spec passes `FORMAT_CONTRACT.json` and originality/safety review.
- Any paid generation stays within the documented retry budget.

## Before owner review

Inspect the exact render for:

- 9:16 framing and duration;
- immediate visual comprehension;
- no speech or linguistic dependence;
- no readable text, labels, logos, or watermarks;
- stable characters, objects, anatomy, and scene continuity;
- a visible buildup, surprising reversal, and satisfying release;
- a clear ending that loops naturally;
- acceptable music/effects and a story that still works muted;
- no safety, cruelty, humiliation, or dangerous-imitation issue.

## Before public posting

- Show the exact video and proposed metadata to the owner.
- Obtain explicit approval for that render.
- Verify the Metricool brand and every connected destination account.
- Use platform copy outside the video only; the video pixels remain language-free.
- Confirm the final channel set, schedule, AI-content disclosure, and tracking parameters.
- Publish only once after a successful verification. Never retry an uncertain submission blindly.

## After posting

Record the platform and post ID, exact render hash, publish time, retention, completion, rewatches,
shares, saves, comments, profile actions, owner notes, and reusable format lessons.
