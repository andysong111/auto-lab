# LoopJolt — Short-form Video Pack v1

## Purpose
Create vertical 9:16 acquisition creatives for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok. Every video must sell the challenge, not the platform.

## Shared rules
- 9:16, 1080x1920
- 10–15 seconds
- Hook in first 1.0s
- Show real LoopJolt game UI or a faithful screen capture; do not invent gameplay mechanics
- No long intro/logo animation
- English copy
- End card 1.5–2.0s: **PLAY FREE · @playloopjolt**
- CTA URL uses platform-specific UTM
- Fast pacing, clean captions, no voice required for v1
- Create one master video per game, then platform-specific exports

## Creative 1 — Don't Press
Hook: **DON'T PRESS THE RED BUTTON.**
0–2s: Big red button, countdown 30.0.
2–8s: Button jumps; PRESS ME / JUST ONCE / I DARE YOU.
8–11s: Near-tap, temptation meter climbs.
11–13s: FAILED at ~12s or WIN at 30.0s.
13–15s: **Can you last 30 seconds? · PLAY FREE**

## Decision rule
- Do not judge by views alone.
- Compare click-through, game_start/session, finish/start, replay/start.
- Winning creative/game gets 3 hook variants.
- Weak views + good game engagement = distribution/creative problem.
- Good views + weak game starts = hook mismatch.
- Good starts + weak replay = game quality problem.
