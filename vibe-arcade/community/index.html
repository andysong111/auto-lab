HOW OVERALL WORKS

Different games. One championship.

Raw scores from different games are never added together. Each ranked game awards placement points: 1st 1,000, 2nd 990, down to 100th 10. Your overall points are the sum across ranked games.

Overall country standings sum the overall championship points of up to 10 distinct players from that country. Game-specific country standings sum the top 10 players’ best raw scores for that game.
