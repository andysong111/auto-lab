How scoring works

Choose a game above to see its scoring rule.

All-games points come from placements: first place earns 1,000, second 990, down to 100th 10. Equal scores share a placement. These points can change as others improve.

Country totals count up to 10 distinct players. Per-game country scores use their best game scores; all-games country scores use their championship totals. Repeated attempts do not add extra points. Countries with fewer contributors can be at a disadvantage.

Weekly boards reset Monday at 00:00 UTC. Per-game records keep the flag used for that run; all-games standings use your current selected flag. Flags are self-selected, not verified nationality. This is score-based competition, not live multiplayer.
