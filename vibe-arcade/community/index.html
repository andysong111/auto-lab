How rankings and country points work

Each game counts one best positive verified score per player. Individual overall points use game placements: 1st 1,000, 2nd 990, down to 100th 10. Tied scores share a placement. Points may change as others improve.

A game-specific country board sums the best raw scores of its top 10 players. The overall country board sums up to 10 players' championship totals. Repeated attempts are not added together. Overall points use each player's current selected country; individual game records retain the country used for that run.

Weekly results reset Monday at 00:00 UTC. Countries with fewer contributors may be at a disadvantage. Flags are self-selected, not verified nationality. This is asynchronous score competition, not live multiplayer.
