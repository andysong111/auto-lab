Guest play and optional accounts

You can play without an account. Ranked beta accounts are for players aged 16 or older. Google sign-in authenticates your game profile. We do not request access to Gmail, contacts, Drive or social accounts.

What is stored

Guest personal bests and sound preferences stay in your browser. Your Google ID token is kept for its limited lifetime in this tab's session storage. The server stores a hashed Google subject identifier, chosen public player ID, optional country or region, consent version, run timestamps and validated scores. Google email addresses, real names and photos are not copied to public scoreboards.

Public player information

Your player ID, selected flag and country code, score and score timestamp appear on public boards after ranked submission. Country is self-selected, not verified nationality, residency or location. Country changes are limited to once every 30 days. Game-specific records retain the flag at run start. All-game Championship standings assign you to your current profile country so you cannot count for multiple countries simultaneously.

Technical and campaign data

Basic game events and referral/campaign information are sent to hosting logs. New editions keep a random tab-session identifier and campaign attribution in session storage to measure navigation from a profile link to a game. Personal bests are saved locally. Hosting and database providers process request metadata to secure and operate the site. Google Identity Services loads when sign-in is requested. No precise location permission is required.

Competition rules

Orbit Sprint and Arcade 3 editions use shared weekly seeds and version-specific rules. The server replays controls and computes scores instead of trusting a claimed score. Only completed signed-in runs may be submitted; pausing, switching tabs or capture mode makes an attempt practice-only. Positive scores enter leaderboards. Each player contributes their best run per game. Country game standings use up to 10 distinct players. All-game points use per-game placement points rather than raw score sums; country Championship standings use up to 10 players' overall points. Smaller participation groups may be disadvantaged. Replay checks do not guarantee that a human played. No cash prizes, betting or paid power-ups are offered.

Retention and deletion

Your profile and published scores remain until account deletion or removal for abuse or service maintenance. “Delete game account” removes your LoopJolt profile and associated scores, not your Google account. Expired competition sessions are cleaned up on subsequent activity; hosting logs follow provider retention settings. Clearing browser data removes local personal bests and sign-in state.

Contact

Contact the brand account @playloopjolt on Instagram or Threads for LoopJolt questions. Never send passwords or Google ID tokens in a message.
