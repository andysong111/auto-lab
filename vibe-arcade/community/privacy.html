Guest play and optional accounts

You can play without creating an account. Ranked beta accounts are for players aged 16 or older. Google sign-in is used only to authenticate your game account. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or social accounts.

What is stored

Guest personal bests and sound preferences stay in your browser. When you choose to sign in, your Google ID token is stored for its limited lifetime in this tab's session storage. On the server, LoopJolt stores a hashed Google subject identifier, your chosen player ID, optional country or region, consent version, run timestamps and validated scores. Google email addresses, real names and photos are not copied into the public scoreboard.

Public player information

Your player ID, selected flag and country code, score and score timestamp appear on public boards after you submit a ranked run. The flag is self-selected: it is not verified nationality, residency or physical location. Country changes are limited to once every 30 days; existing scores retain the country selected for that run.

Technical data

The site sends basic game events and campaign attribution to its hosting logs. Hosting and database providers process technical request metadata to run and secure the service. Google sign-in loads Google Identity Services when requested. LoopJolt does not require precise location permission.

Competition rules

Orbit Sprint runs share one course each UTC week. The server replays submitted controls and computes the score; your device's claimed score is not trusted. Only completed signed-in runs can enter rankings. Pausing, switching tabs or losing the page makes the run practice-only. Each person has one best score per board. Country standings sum the best scores of up to 10 distinct players, not repeat attempts. Countries with fewer contributors may be at a disadvantage. No cash prizes, betting or paid power-ups are offered.

Retention and deletion

Your profile and published scores remain until you delete your LoopJolt account or records are removed for abuse or service maintenance. Use “Delete game account” on the player page while signed in to remove your game profile and associated scores. This does not delete your Google account. Expired competition sessions are cleaned up on subsequent activity; hosting logs follow the hosting provider's retention settings. Clearing browser data removes local personal bests and sign-in state.

Contact

For LoopJolt questions, contact the brand account @playloopjolt on Instagram or Threads. Never send passwords or Google ID tokens in a message.
