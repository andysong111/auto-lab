## Editorial synthesis — an experiment, not a proven recipe
- Completion challenge: convert 'can this difficult game be completed?' into 'Can YOUR flag clear all 48?'.
- Obstacle anticipation and payoff: show a real closing/opening gate, then a real successful drop and charged break-through. Do not fabricate fail rates such as '99% fail'.
- Visually distinct stages: cut between the actual sanctuary, crystal vault and ember engine instead of repeating one ring.
- LoopJolt identity: country flag choices + in-game verified-score CTA. Flags are invitations to participate, not pretend competitors or a fabricated match result.

The finished first film is titled **Can Your Flag Clear All 48? | Deep Descent**. It is a country-leaderboard participation challenge, not real-time multiplayer, an official national-team tournament, a cash-prize event or proof of existing international participation.

## Operating rule for future SNS
Research before creating: record reference title, channel, publication date, current public view count, observed/inferred format and source. Include short-form examples where possible. Copy a storytelling mechanism, never another creator's footage or game IP. Build a game-specific participation hook around a real LoopJolt mechanic and a verified-score/country CTA. Keep practice demos explicitly separate from genuine user submissions. Until real scores from multiple countries exist, use invitations/challenges, not fabricated country-versus-country recaps. After sufficient genuine records, comparisons must match game version, weekly course and period; use public handles only and appropriate permission for submitted footage.

No new subscriptions, purchased assets, paid AI generations or ad spend. Existing media/hosting service usage may still carry normal usage charges. No game code, database, auth configuration or existing leaderboard record was changed. No performance, retention, revenue or conversion lift is claimed at publication time.
