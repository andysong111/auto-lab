## Scoring copy must match the product
The live API says a game-country board sums the best runs of up to10 distinct players. Championship totals sum per-game placement points for each player, then up to10 players per country; raw scores across games are NOT added directly.
Consequently: no 'every attempt adds points', 'comment a flag to score', 'clearing48 automatically wins for your country', or 'a run worth the displayed deficit will definitely overtake'. A repeated lower score may change nothing. Say: 'Set your best. Compete for your flag.' Show a national gain/position change only after corresponding real before/after board reads. Ties, excluded contributors and rank-point shifts must be respected.

## Format B — ONE MORE RUN FOR YOUR FLAG
Ready for second production after fresh capture QA. Game: Core Pins, to avoid repeating the same falling-game look.
Source pattern: meaningful failure -> immediate retry -> clean success, based on Stage1's Happy Wheels retry/surprise observations. No copied gore, audio or streamer personality.
0–2s: Two or three equal flag badges, real rotating core already moving. Hook: 'REP YOUR FLAG.'
2–6s: Actual collision visible and its real consequence. Copy: 'TOO EARLY.' A lost shield is not falsely presented as game over.
6–10s: Corrected timing/clean throw. If it is another attempt, mark the cut 'NEXT TRY'; otherwise do not fake a restart. Copy: 'ONE MORE TRY.'
10–15s: Brief genuine practice score (marked practice) or verified result (only if real). CTA: 'SET YOUR BEST. BACK YOUR FLAG.'
