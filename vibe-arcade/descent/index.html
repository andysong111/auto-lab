How to descend

Rotate, don't aim the drone. Drag left/right or hold the arrow keys. Your drone always contacts the front of the ring, directly beneath it. The glowing edges mark an opening.

Earn your burst. Clean drops fill the meter; centered drops earn more. At 100, press Space or BURST to break the next three floors. A burst is consumed and cannot recharge itself.

Learn the vaults. Moving rings drift independently. Cracked stone breaks after two safe bounces. Red gates open blue periodically. Focus crystals slow moving obstacles, not the game timer. Every 16 rings a guardian gate leads to a new world and repairs one shield.

48 rings · 12 sectors · 90 seconds. Unpaused, server-verified runs use a new rules-v2 board. Old Gyro Drop scores remain stored separately; practice records are local. No cash prizes or paid power-ups.

Guardians seal the way at rings 16, 32 and 48. Break one, two and three seals respectively. Each broken seal moves the opening. A full burst breaks only one guardian seal.
