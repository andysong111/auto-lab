FIELD MANUAL Controls, evolution & records +

MOVE TO SURVIVE. Use WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere in the arena with one finger. Your lance automatically aims at the nearest defender. Space or PHASE DASH gives a short invulnerable burst in your last movement direction. P or Escape pauses.

EVERY CLEAR CHANGES YOU. Defeat every defender at a gate. Your frame immediately gains armor, bolt damage, +6 maximum hull and up to 24 repair. Pick one of three module upgrades before the next gate. Companion drones arrive at frame tiers 3 and 6. Upgrades stack during this expedition.

LEARN THE GUARDIANS. Gates 3, 6 and 9 have unique guardians. Watch the red aiming lines, expanding rings and marked impact zones. Later guardians gain extra attacks below half hull. Green shards restore 10 hull. Phase through bullets, not into the next attack.

A FAIR, FRESH RUN. A new expedition starts at gate 1 with a Scout frame. Upgrades reset when you retry; device best and furthest cleared gate remain on this browser when storage is available. These are local records, not verified world or country scores. No pay-to-win, ads or account required. Maximum active expedition time: 12 minutes.
