How to play & options
THE FLIGHT MANUAL

Easy to enter.
Harder to master.

↔
Find a clear lane

Left / Right or A / D. Tall pillars must be dodged unless dashing.

↑
Time your jump

Space, W or swipe up. Jump over low barriers, not tall pillars.

✦
Build your energy

Collect 5 orbs, then press X or DASH. A dash breaks obstacles for a short time.

Collect orbs in consecutive rows for a bigger bonus. The course accelerates as you survive.

Only completed ranked runs enter the official boards. Pausing, switching tabs or unstable frame timing turns a run into practice. Device bests are separate.
