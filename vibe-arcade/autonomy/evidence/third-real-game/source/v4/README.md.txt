# Ribbon Shift

Ribbon Shift is an original toroidal-strip spatial puzzle. Restore three woven 3×3 mosaics by moving a whole row right or a whole column down. Every shift wraps at the far edge; tiles never merge.

Use Left and Right to choose one of the six handles, then press Space to shift that strip. On touch screens, tap any row (or its right arrow) to move that row right, or tap a bottom arrow to move its column down.

Each deterministic seed creates three solvable boards with exact shortest solution depths of two, three, and four moves. Every selected shortest route uses both row and column shifts. Replay returns to the same seeded weave. Current score and device-local practice best are supplied by GameKit.
